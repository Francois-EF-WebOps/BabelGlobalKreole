# Evidence, provenance, rights and correction policy

## Evidence labels
- `attested`: directly supported by a traceable source for stated language/variety/time/register/meaning.
- `scholarly_analysis`: analysis attributable to a named source.
- `comparative_observation`: documented comparison; not by itself an origin claim.
- `proposed_convergence`: design proposal, not established community usage.
- `creative_coinage`: intentionally invented for a named creative/constructed-language project.
- `application_canon`: accepted within a named fictional/application context.
- `disputed`: credible sources or contributors conflict.
- `undecided`: evidence or decision remains insufficient.
- `retracted`: withdrawn/corrected record with audit trail.

## Provenance minimum
Record source ID, title, creator/institution where known, stable URL/identifier, publication/version and access dates, relevant page/section, source language, licence/terms, attribution requirements, permitted operations, and integrity hash if a local copy is legitimately held. Derived records name parent source IDs, method, contributor/tool where appropriate and review status.

## Rights gates
A public URL is not permission to redistribute. Check licence compatibility for the exact use. Separate source metadata from protected expression such as dictionary definitions, example sentences, recordings and curated datasets. Unknown rights means do not redistribute content. Never publish secrets, sensitive personal data or non-consensual recordings.

## Uncertainty and corrections
Confidence does not replace evidence. Corrections record prior claim, corrected claim, reason, evidence, date, reviewer and affected records. Preserve history unless legal, privacy or safety obligations require removal; document removals without exposing removed material.

Credit speakers, researchers and contributors according to source terms and informed consent. Do not imply endorsement or collective authorship without authorization.
