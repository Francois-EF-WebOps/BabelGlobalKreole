**Reviewed:** 2026-10-17  
**Purpose:** Track names considered for coverage without manufacturing language records or identifiers. A row here is not an accepted language/variety classification.

The data directory contains only candidates with an inspected Glottolog catalogue page and verified identifiers. Glottolog catalogue labels and codes establish the catalogue identity and identifiers recorded in each source record; they do not, by themselves, settle lexifier history, community identity, dialect boundaries, or consent for downstream uses.

## Remaining evidence work

- Independently fetch APiCS pages for each candidate where Glottolog lists an APiCS link, record the canonical URL, date, and relevant location, and retain link-only use unless the exact permission and consent basis for a proposed transformation are clear.
- Resolve Dominican Kwéyòl and island-specific Cape Verdean varieties with their own source pages before creating variety records.
- Keep Papiamento's lexifier claim disputed; do not collapse Spanish and Portuguese hypotheses into a single asserted lexifier.
- Keep Michif and Nubi explicitly marked as scope extensions. Other languages with non-European lexifiers or mixed-language status should be treated similarly after evidence review.
- A language catalogue record is not a lexical or grammar record. No lexical/grammar examples should be added from memory or copied from dictionaries without rights review.
