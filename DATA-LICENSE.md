# Data and documentation licensing

The repository's `LICENSE` file applies to code unless otherwise stated. It must not be interpreted as granting rights in third-party source material, quoted examples, recordings, dictionaries, corpora, or community-held knowledge.

For original, independently authored framework data released by the maintainers, the intended default is **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** only where the maintainers have authority to license it. The license text is available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

Every source-derived record must retain its own rights status, license/terms, attribution requirements, and permitted operations. A source with unknown, restricted, or permission-pending rights must not be redistributed merely because it is included in this repository. A source's license may impose conditions that differ from this default. Do not apply the default to material where ownership, authority, or community consent is unclear.

For AI-related operations, distinguish at least:
- retrieval/display for a specific purpose;
- redistribution of source or derived content;
- model training or fine-tuning;
- speech generation or voice cloning;
- synthetic-data generation based on the source;
- retention and onward sharing.

Permission for one operation does not imply permission for the others. Community consent and legal rights are separate questions. This policy is not legal advice; unresolved cases require human review.
