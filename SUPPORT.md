# Support

BabelGlobalKreole is maintained by one person on a best-effort basis. There is no paid support, guaranteed response time, or service-level agreement.

- For a data correction, use the correction issue form and provide the record ID, source URL, and precise source location.
- For a new external resource, use the resource suggestion form and include official license/terms evidence where available.
- For an unsupported or misleading AI answer, use the bad-AI-answer report form with the question, answer, cited record IDs, and relevant evidence.
- For general design discussion, open a focused issue with a clear question and acceptance criteria.

Do not post private conversation exports, personal data, credentials, non-consensual recordings, or third-party copyrighted text unless you have authority to share it. A link or citation does not grant reuse permission.
