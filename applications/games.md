# Games application contract

For a constructed/in-world language such as Mooritian in *Fresh: Love and Water*, separate lexical/grammar data; dialogue intent; character knowledge and beliefs; world state and action outcomes; subtitles, voice and accessibility; fictional canon and its decision log.

A character's ability to speak does not grant survival knowledge or professional skill. Hearing a term does not automatically make a character believe it or learn a skill. Real-language sources can inform design but do not become fictional canon automatically.

## Register metadata

Register is optional and variety-specific. If a project records a register description (for example, a community- or scholar-used term such as *basilect*, *mesolect* or *acrolect*), store it as sourced free text with source IDs and a scope note. Do not force every Creole into a three-part continuum, and do not treat these labels as a universal enum. If no source supports the label for the specific language/variety, omit it.
