# Grammar records

Grammar claims are paraphrased, tightly scoped, and linked to a source ID plus a precise source location. The initial records prioritize Kreol Morisien and link to APiCS Online. They do not reproduce source examples, tables, or extended prose.

A source URL alone does not authorize redistribution or AI training. Current AI-use and community-consent flags remain unknown unless separately verified. Do not add grammar claims from memory; do not generalize a claim across varieties without evidence.
