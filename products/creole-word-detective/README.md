# Creole Word Detective — Source-Backed Learning Cards

**Format:** self-contained printable HTML worksheet pack  
**Audience:** language learners, families, teachers and community language projects  
**Purpose:** teach learners to collect and compare words responsibly, with evidence and attribution, without inventing translations or treating one spelling as universal.

## Product contents
- `creole-word-detective.html` — four A4 printable pages: source log, word card, comparison card and review checklist.
- The pack deliberately contains no pre-filled vocabulary or unverified translations. Learners fill it only from sources they are allowed to use.

## How to use
1. Choose a language/variety and a specific learning question.
2. Locate a reliable dictionary, grammar, recording, publication or speaker-reviewed source.
3. Record the exact source location and its rights/terms before copying any material.
4. Keep the source's spelling and wording distinct from your own notes.
5. Mark uncertainty and variation; do not assume similar-looking words share an origin.
6. Ask a qualified speaker or language specialist to review learner-produced content before public distribution.

## Rights and evidence
This is an original blank educational worksheet, not a lexical dataset. The repository's data policy does not grant blanket permission to republish third-party dictionary or corpus entries. Do not add sourced words to a commercial edition until the source terms permit that use. Community consent and legal reuse rights are separate considerations.
