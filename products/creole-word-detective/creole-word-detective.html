Detective rule

Write down what a source actually says. Keep your own interpretation separate. “I don’t know yet” is a valid research result.

Choose your source carefully

A source being online does not mean it is free to copy. Record the licence or terms before reproducing words, audio, images or definitions.

Evidence & respect checklist

Every copied or paraphrased claim has a traceable source.

Source wording is distinct from my own explanation.

Spelling and variety labels are not silently normalised.

Uncertainty, disagreement and missing evidence are recorded.

Rights, attribution and permitted use have been checked.

A competent speaker or language specialist reviewed public-facing content.

No personal recording or community-held knowledge is published without appropriate consent.
