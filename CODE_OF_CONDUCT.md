# Community participation

BabelGlobalKreole welcomes respectful participation from speakers, researchers, educators, archivists, developers, artists, and other contributors.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people with respect; critique claims and methods without harassment or personal attacks.
- Be honest about evidence, uncertainty, expertise, and the limits of your own knowledge.
- Do not claim to speak for a community without a verifiable mandate.
- Respect speakers' choices about naming, orthography, recording, attribution, access, and reuse.
- Do not infer consent to publish, redistribute, train models, synthesize speech, or generate derivative material.
- Preserve meaningful disagreement; do not pressure people to manufacture consensus.
- Keep private information private and avoid discriminatory, threatening, or sexually harassing conduct.

## Handling concerns

Report a concern through a private contact route available from the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in public issues. The maintainer will review concerns in good faith, protect privacy where practicable, and may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate these expectations.

This is a maintainer-led project, not a constituted community body. The policy does not imply endorsement by any language community.
