# Governance

## Current status
This is a maintainer-led draft framework. It does not claim a constituted standards body, community mandate, formal membership, or endorsement by Creole-language communities.

## Decision classes
- **Framework mechanics:** schemas, IDs, validation scripts; maintainer decision with documented review.
- **Evidence claims:** source-backed review; specialist/community input when feasible.
- **Convergence proposals:** explicitly proposed; alternatives and affected communities recorded.
- **Fictional canon:** decided by the named application owner and labelled as fictional.
- **Rights/privacy:** conservative gate; no release while unresolved.
- **Release/version:** breaking changes require migration notes and downstream impact review.

## Safeguards
Publish disagreement and uncertainty rather than manufacturing consensus. Invite speakers and researchers to correct descriptions where practicable. Distinguish community usage from individual proposals. Do not claim endorsement without verifiable evidence. Provide a correction path and review requests for personal or sensitive material.

## Conflict handling
Preserve credible competing claims with scope and sources. Record what evidence would resolve the conflict. Label unresolved matters disputed or undecided. Rights, privacy and harm concerns take precedence over stylistic preference.

## Recognition and adoption
Track external issues, citations, forks, reuse, corrections, community review and independently reported outcomes. Downloads, generated output volume and social impressions alone do not establish linguistic validity or adoption.
